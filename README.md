# Doctrine and Covenants Geography (~1805 – 1890)
### Interactive Historical Atlas & Chronological Codex

An interactive, high-fidelity cartographic atlas and chronological explorer of the **Doctrine and Covenants** and the history of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints from the birth of the Prophet Joseph Smith in 1805 through the 1890 Manifesto.

Built to complement its sister scriptural atlases:
- [Old Testament Geography - Interactive Biblical Atlas (~4000 BC – 400 BC)](https://jviola60.github.io/old-testament-geography/)
- [New Testament Geography - Interactive Atlas (~6 BC – 100 AD)](https://jviola60.github.io/new-testament-geography/)
- [Book of Mormon Geography - Interactive Atlas & Codex (~2200 BC – AD 421)](https://jviola60.github.io/book-of-mormon-geography/)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Strictly Chronological Sequence**:
   - While the canonical Doctrine and Covenants sections are not arranged strictly chronologically (e.g. Section 1 was received in November 1831 at Hiram, Ohio, while Section 2 was received in September 1823 in Manchester, NY), this atlas arranges every revelation and foundational event in **exact historical chronological order**.
   - Scrub through time from 1805 to 1890 with live map synchronization and step-by-step narration.

2. **Dual Primary Sources & Archival Cross-References**:
   - Direct links to authoritative scripture text on [ChurchofJesusChrist.org](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament).
   - Direct links to original manuscript scans and historical introductions on [JosephSmithPapers.org](https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/).

3. **100+ Authentic Historic Locations**:
   - Precision coordinates for sacred sites, homes, farms, printing offices, meetinghouses, jails, and temple sites across Vermont, New York, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Missouri, Illinois, Iowa, Nebraska, Wyoming, and Utah.

4. **Historic Trails & Expeditions**:
   - Zion's Camp March (1834) from Kirtland, Ohio to Clay County, Missouri.
   - The Mormon Pioneer Trail (1846–1847) from Nauvoo through Iowa waystations, Winter Quarters, and the Rocky Mountain passes to the Salt Lake Valley.
   - Early missionary journeys to the Lamanites (1830) and the British Isles (1837).

5. **Desktop & Mobile Optimized**:
   - Full desktop experience with multi-pane codex, search autocomplete, region jump, and pioneer distance calculator.
   - Mobile-first responsive app shell with touch gestures, bottom sheet codex, and map-first ergonomics.

6. **Rich Archival 19th-Century Aesthetics**:
   - Styled after early American letterpress typography and antique leather-bound journals reminiscent of the Joseph Smith Papers project.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Core**: Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, modern ES6 JavaScript.
- **Cartography**: [Leaflet.js](https://leafletjs.com/) with Antiquarian Shaded Relief, Satellite Earth Terrain, and Modern Map layer options.
- **Typography**: Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond, Inter, and Outfit via Google Fonts.
- **Zero Heavy Build Dependencies**: Runs cleanly on GitHub Pages, local dev servers, or static file hosts.
